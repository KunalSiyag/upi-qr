**UPI payment receipt generator** searches usually mean one of two jobs. Shops and freelancers need a PDF that proves money arrived. A smaller set of queries want a fake PhonePe, GPay, or Paytm success screen. This page only does the first job.

A receipt is a merchant document: payer, amount, date, mode, and UTR. It is not an app screenshot, and it does not move money.

*Last reviewed 13 September 2026.*

## Issue a receipt after the credit lands

1. Confirm the amount in the bank SMS or passbook — not only the green tile in PhonePe or GPay.
2. Copy the **12-digit UTR**.
3. Open the [payment receipt generator](/receipt-generator/).
4. Enter payer name, amount, date, payment mode (UPI), and UTR. Add a UPI QR if you want the next invoice paid by scan.
5. Download the PDF. Stamp PAID if you already sent an invoice — [paid stamp tool](/paid-stamp-generator/).

Everything runs in the browser. No GST return is filed for you.

## PhonePe / GPay / Paytm receipts vs a merchant receipt

| | In-app history | Merchant receipt PDF |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who issues it | The payer’s UPI app | You, after the credit |
| What it proves | That their app showed success | That you acknowledged the UTR |
| Fake-able? | Screenshot-spoof apps exist | Still forgeable — pair with bank SMS |
| Use | Payer’s records | Your books, HRA, client handover |

We do not generate PhonePe, Google Pay, or Paytm success screens. Those are app UI. Asking for a “PhonePe receipt generator” that looks like the official tile is how screenshot fraud starts. If you need proof as the **payer**, open the transaction in the app and export or screenshot *your* history — do not download a template that pretends to be PhonePe.

For rent, use the [rent receipt generator](/rent-receipt-generator/) (HRA fields, amount in words, revenue-stamp box). For GST invoices that *ask* for money, use the [invoice generator](/invoice-generator/) and send a receipt only after settlement. See [quotation vs invoice vs receipt](/blog/quotation-vs-estimate-vs-invoice-vs-receipt/).

## What to print on a UPI receipt

- Receipt number and date
- Your name / shop name and the payer’s name
- Amount in figures and words
- Mode: UPI, plus the VPA that received it
- UTR
- Optional next-payment QR from the [UPI QR generator](/)

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

### Q1. Can I generate a PhonePe or GPay payment receipt?

You can generate a **merchant** receipt that records a PhonePe or GPay UPI credit, including the UTR. You cannot get an official PhonePe/GPay PDF from this site, and we will not draw a fake success screen.

### Q2. Is a receipt the same as a UTR?

No. The UTR is the bank’s reference. The receipt is your acknowledgement that you received that UTR. Keep both.

### Q3. Do I need GST on a UPI receipt?

A receipt does not replace a tax invoice. If you are GST-registered, issue the invoice first, then the receipt. Composition dealers may need a bill of supply instead.

### Q4. Can I download a UPI payment receipt for rent?

Yes — use the [rent receipt generator](/rent-receipt-generator/) so HRA fields and the stamp box are present. A generic shop receipt is the wrong format for payroll.
