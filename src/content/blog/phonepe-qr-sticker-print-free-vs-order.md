**PhonePe QR code sticker order online free** is two products glued together. PhonePe Business can ship an official merchant sticker after KYC. You can also print a **compatible** UPI sticker today from the VPA you already own. Customers can pay either one from PhonePe — the difference is dashboard, soundbox, and branding, not the rail.

## Print a free sticker this afternoon

1. Copy your VPA from PhonePe (Profile → UPI IDs) or any bank app.
2. Open the [A4 QR sticker sheet generator](/qr-sticker-generator/) or the [PhonePe-compatible QR](/phonepe-qr-generator/).
3. Export PDF at the physical size you will cut. Do not stretch a screenshot.
4. Print on **matte vinyl** if it goes on glass; paper is fine for a dry indoor till.
5. Test-scan with PhonePe and GPay. Decode with the [UPI QR decoder](/upi-qr-decoder/) so `pa=` is yours.

A4 sheets print 4, 6, or 12 stickers. Full layout notes: [printable UPI QR sticker sheet guide](/blog/printable-upi-qr-sticker-sheet-guide/). Outdoor tea stalls need [waterproof vinyl](/blog/how-to-print-durable-waterproof-qr-stickers/).

This print is **not** PhonePe’s official merchant kit. It will not enrol a soundbox or Maps listing.

## When to order the official PhonePe sticker

Order from **PhonePe Business** when you want KYC, settlement reports, or a rented speaker. Activation steps: [PhonePe Business QR code activation](/blog/phonepe-business-qr-code-activation/).

| | Print here (free) | Order in PhonePe Business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost | Paper/vinyl + ink | In-app; often free for the first sticker, confirm in the app |
| Ready | Same day | Courier wait |
| Who can pay | Any UPI app | Any UPI app |
| Soundbox / dashboard | No | Yes, after KYC |
| Reprint after VPA change | Immediate | Re-order or reprint a compatible sheet |

If the in-app order is paid or delayed, print the compatible sheet for the counter and keep the official sticker for the door once it arrives.

## Sticker paper, size, and fraud

- Counter QR: at least 2.5–3 cm on the short side. [Size chart](/blog/upi-qr-code-size-dimensions-printing-guide/).
- Avoid gloss lamination — glare is the usual “PhonePe sticker not scanning” complaint.
- Overlay stickers are the common swap fraud. Prefer an acrylic standee over a loose paper square. Inspect daily.

Hardware if you do not want to cut sheets: [waterproof QR stickers](/products/waterproof-qr-stickers/) and [acrylic standee](/products/acrylic-qr-standee/) (Amazon Associate links labelled).

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

### Q1. Can I order a PhonePe QR sticker online for free?

PhonePe Business lets merchants request a physical QR after KYC; whether that sticker is free is an in-app offer, not a guarantee. A compatible sticker from your VPA is free to print today.

### Q2. Will GPay and Paytm users pay a PhonePe sticker?

Yes, if it encodes a live `upi://pay` VPA. Branding does not lock the app.

### Q3. Paper sticker or vinyl?

Paper for a dry indoor till you can replace. Matte vinyl for glass, tiffins, and outdoor counters.

### Q4. How do I get a UPI sticker for the shop without PhonePe Business?

Generate the sheet, print, test-scan. You do not need a merchant app for a personal or current-account VPA. Read [UPI QR without a business account](/blog/how-to-create-upi-qr-without-business-account/).
