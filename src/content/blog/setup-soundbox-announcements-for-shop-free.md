If you sit at the desk all day, you do not need to rent a soundbox to hear “₹50 received”. PhonePe Business and Google Pay for Business can speak the amount from the **phone speaker**. Keep volume up, keep the phone on charge, and treat silence like an unconfirmed payment.

*Last reviewed 27 August 2026. Toggle names move; look for Voice alerts / Audio notifications in Settings.*


<h2 id="phonepe">PhonePe Business (free, in-app)</h2>

1. Open **PhonePe Business** (the merchant app).
2. Profile / menu → **Settings**.
3. Enable **Voice alerts** or **Audio notifications**.
4. Pick a language you actually understand over the fan noise.
5. Play the test clip and leave the app allowed to run in the background (battery optimisation off for this app).

Payments to the PhonePe merchant VPA can then be announced while the phone is on the counter. Setup of the QR itself: [PhonePe Business QR activation](/blog/phonepe-business-qr-code-activation/).

<h2 id="gpay">Google Pay for Business (free, in-app)</h2>

1. Open **Google Pay for Business**.
2. **Settings** → **Notifications**.
3. Enable **Voice notifications** and choose a language.
4. Confirm the test prompt.

GPay activation: [Google Pay for Business setup](/blog/google-pay-business-qr-code-activation/).

<h2 id="limits">When the phone is not enough</h2>

- You leave the counter (kitchen, store room).
- Two staff, one phone, noisy lunch rush.
- Customers flash spoofed success screens and you need a speaker that only talks after the **provider** sees the credit.

Then look at a provider soundbox — and read today’s rent *in the app*, not in an old blog. How the hardware works: [UPI soundboxes, safety, and DIY costs](/blog/how-upi-soundboxes-work-and-their-safety/).

<h2 id="faq">Frequently asked questions</h2>

### Q1. Will PhonePe voice alerts announce GPay customer payments?

If the customer paid **your PhonePe merchant VPA**, PhonePe Business can announce it regardless of which app they used. If they paid a different VPA that only lives in another app, that other app (or bank SMS) is the notifier.

### Q2. Does this cost MDR?

No. Voice is a notification. Bank-to-bank UPI remains 0% MDR. RuPay credit card on UPI above ₹2,000 may carry interchange.

### Q3. Android killed the alerts overnight.

Exempt PhonePe Business / GPay Business from battery optimisation and keep the phone plugged in at the desk. That is the usual failure, not a “UPI outage”.
