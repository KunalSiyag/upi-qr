PNG vs PDF for a printed UPI QR

PNG

Best for WhatsApp and inkjet

  • Raster pixels. Export at the physical size × 300 DPI.
  • Fine for home printers and chat sharing.
  • Do not stretch a small screenshot to A4.

PDF

Safer print-shop master

  • Keeps page size (A4 / A5) without resampling.
  • Better when a shop will reprint later.
  • Still test-scan the first proof copy.

Pro UPI QR does not claim SVG or WebAssembly print engines. Use PNG or PDF from the browser download, then print at 300 DPI on matte stock.
